# Clockwork SMP: The Ultimate Experience
## A custom feature-mod tailored for the Clockwork SMP (C.SMP)

This mod compiles everything added to the C.SMP, bringing unique mechanics, powerful endgame gear, and custom audio features to your world.

---

## Custom Music Discs

Bring your own soundtrack into Minecraft! The mod includes a built-in **MP3-to-Music-Disc Converter** that handles audio formatting automatically:
*   Converts **MP3 (Stereo or Mono)** directly into **OGG (Mono)** format for optimal in-game performance.
*   ⚠️ **Important Note:** This mod *does not* include a music downloader. All MP3 files must already be stored locally on your hard drive.
